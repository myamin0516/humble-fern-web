About

Humble Fern LLC is a North Carolina limited liability company and independent software studio. We make mobile apps with a focus on calm design and practical everyday tools.

Our first product is Grove, a notes app for iPhone. Grove helps you capture ideas and revisit them without noise.

The studio is based in North Carolina, USA. For questions about Humble Fern LLC or Grove, visit Support or email hello@humblefern.com.
